Student Attendance Analytics

An end-to-end reporting module recognized by stakeholders for its technical quality and usability.

ASP.NET SQL Server Scheduled jobs HTML email

School leadership needed attendance insight without logging in and running reports by hand. I built the Student Attendance Summary module end-to-end — part of the HRIS schedule — turning raw attendance data into a report that lands in inboxes every day.

What it does

  • Dynamic HTML report generated from attendance data, styled to be readable in any email client.
  • Scheduled daily email delivery to stakeholders — no manual step.
  • Whole-school, grade-wise, and trend analytics so leaders can spot patterns, not just single-day numbers.

Why the design worked

Reports are only useful if people actually read them. Delivering a clean, pre-computed summary by email — instead of expecting busy staff to pull it themselves — is what drove adoption. It was recognized internally for both its technical quality and its usability.

The technical shape

The heavy lifting happens in SQL — aggregating attendance across the school and by grade, computing trends over time. A scheduled job renders the HTML and dispatches the email each morning. Because the aggregation is done server-side and cached into the report, recipients get a fast, static artifact rather than a slow live query.
