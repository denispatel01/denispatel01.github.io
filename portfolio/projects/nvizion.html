nVizion — US Bank Loan Processing Platform

An end-to-end loan-processing workflow for a US banking client, with borrower identity verification and credit assessment.

ASP.NET Core SQL Server Experian / Equifax IDology FlashSpread Payment gateways

▶ Try the live demo

A US banking client needed loan officers to manage the entire loan lifecycle — from application to approval — through a single web interface, with the compliance- grade identity and credit checks that US lending requires.

What I delivered

  • The end-to-end loan-processing workflow, letting loan officers move an application from intake to approval in one interface.
  • Secure document upload, automated status tracking, and role-based dashboards — cutting manual data-entry touchpoints for loan officers.
  • On the DueDiligence borrower-verification flow, integrated Experian, Equifax, IDology, and FlashSpread for US borrower KYC and credit assessment — including API-key and token-based authentication for each provider.
  • Debugged and maintained existing payment-gateway integrations (Payze, HDFC, Paytm), resolving production payment-flow issues across environments.

Integration challenge

Each verification provider had its own auth scheme, request/response shape, and failure modes. I wrapped them behind a consistent internal contract so the loan workflow didn't care which bureau answered — making the flow easier to test and to extend with new providers.

Working across time zones

This was a remote engagement for a Maryland, USA client through a Microsoft Silver Partner. I collaborated asynchronously across US–India time zones within Agile sprints, consistently meeting delivery milestones — a working style I've kept sharp and one that suits freelance clients well.

Why it mattered

Lending platforms live and die on trust and compliance. Getting KYC and credit integration right — with proper auth, error handling, and auditability — is what let loan officers rely on the system for real approval decisions.
